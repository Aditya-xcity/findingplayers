WHY FINDINGPLAYERS?

Good ideas need
the right people.

Finding a reliable teammate can be harder than coming up with an idea. This platform brings discovery, recruitment, and project coordination into one student-friendly space.

Project status: This page showcases the project concept and starter implementation. Live features depend on the Flask application being deployed separately.
